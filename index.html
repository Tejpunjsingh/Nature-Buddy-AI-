    A note about NatureBuddy 🌿

    NatureBuddy is designed to make the screen the shortest part of an outdoor adventure. Activity ideas, plans, and your field notes work locally. If you run Ollama on your own machine, the site can optionally ask your local open-weight model for a more tailored plan. No hosted AI API is needed.

    Take care outside: use familiar public places, check conditions yourself, respect plants and wildlife, and put your phone away while moving. Plans are ideas, not live local safety or weather information.
